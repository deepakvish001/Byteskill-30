## Purpose {#purpose}
Byteskill aims to be a go-to resource for anyone looking to learn or deepen their understanding of programming, software engineering, artificial intelligence, machine learning, data science, competitive programming, and core computer science principles.

## Technology Stack {#tech-stack}
*   **Framework:** Next.js (App Router)
*   **Styling:** Tailwind CSS
*   **Content:** MDX (Markdown with JSX)
*   **UI Components:** Shadcn/UI (adapted)
*   **Icons:** Lucide React
*   **Theme Management:** `next-themes`
*   **Deployment:** Vercel

## Content Focus {#content-focus}
The content on Byteskill is curated to be:
*   **Accurate and Up-to-Date:** Reflecting current best practices and technologies.
*   **Clear and Concise:** Making complex topics understandable.
*   **Practical and Actionable:** Providing examples and guidance you can apply.
*   **Engaging:** Fostering a love for learning and problem-solving.

We are constantly working to expand and improve the Byteskill platform.
